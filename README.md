Semiconductor Factor Model

Overview

This project explores a quantitative factor model focused on the semiconductor industry. The objective was to analyze how different financial, macroeconomic, and industry-specific factors influence semiconductor stock performance and market behavior.

The project combines financial analysis, data analysis, and investment research techniques to build a framework for evaluating semiconductor companies from both a quantitative and strategic perspective.

---

Project Objectives

- Analyze semiconductor sector performance using factor investing principles
- Identify drivers of returns across semiconductor equities
- Compare growth, valuation, momentum, and profitability metrics
- Explore relationships between AI demand, optics, GPUs, memory, and chip manufacturing trends
- Build a scalable framework for future quantitative finance research

---

Key Insights

- AI infrastructure demand is reshaping semiconductor capital allocation.
- Optical networking and photonics are becoming increasingly important as data transfer speeds scale beyond traditional copper limitations.
- Semiconductor leadership cycles are strongly influenced by innovation timing, supply chain positioning, and institutional capital flows.
- Momentum and profitability factors often dominate during AI-driven market expansions.
